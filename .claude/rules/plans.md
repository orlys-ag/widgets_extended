# Working in `plans/`

Plans and the files of a feature-delivery run, under `plans/delivery/`, follow
`doc/agents/delivery-method.md`: read it before writing, auditing or revising a
plan.

The other plans in `plans/`, and the records beside them, were written to the
retired method, `plans/AUDIT-METHOD.md` (git history keeps it), some of them
by runs of the retired `feature-implementation` workflow. They are history: a
source change does not repoint their citations, and continuing one of their
plans is a feature-delivery run whose requirements name it.
